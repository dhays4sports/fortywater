# 408FARMERS Campaign Creative Refresh Specification

Build: **408-UI-3.11.1**

## Purpose
Refresh future paid and physical creative so it feels like the same brand as UI-3 while preserving proven profession-first and situation-first hooks.

## Occupational creative system
Use:
- current 408FARMERS wordmark
- 408 navy, white, Farmers red
- profession-specific photography
- clean sans-serif typography
- one primary hook
- one eligibility statement
- `CHECK MY ELIGIBILITY`
- official Farmers credential treatment
- required eligibility/disclosure language

Preserve these hooks:
- **Work in Tech?**
- **Are You a Teacher?**
- **Are You an Engineer?**
- **Work in Healthcare?**

Avoid rebuilding the website around the legacy gold creative treatment. Gold may appear only if separately approved as a campaign accent; it is not the UI-3 platform action color.

## Home / physical creative
Preserve the direct-response hook and CTA while allowing the landing page to remain in UI-3 Home mode.

Front coaster:
- `OWN A HOME IN THE SOUTH BAY?`
- `GET MY COVERAGE REVIEW`

Back coaster:
- `OWN THE HOME. DRIVE THE CARS. REVIEW THEM TOGETHER.`
- `GET MY HOME + AUTO REVIEW`

## Buyer / realtor creative
Keep the partner/realtor promise focused on closing context. The landing page should recognize explicit partner-card traffic but continue into the certified Buyer flow.

## Life creative
Life is the deliberate exception. Preserve deeper campaign-to-page visual continuity: cinematic black/deep navy, white declaration typography, Farmers red timing accents, warm family photography, and Life blue where useful.

## Compliance boundary
Campaign creative may describe potential professional discounts only as conditional and subject to eligibility / policy terms. No creative may promise savings, a lower rate, eligibility, underwriting approval, or a policy outcome.
