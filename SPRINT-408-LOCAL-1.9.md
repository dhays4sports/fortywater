# 408-LOCAL-1.9 — Pilot Merchant Launch

## Outcome
The Local pilot has moved from an empty pre-launch directory to its first real merchant activation. Stevie's Bar & Grill is now a public Eat & Drink merchant with a merchant-owned, show-your-screen Local perk. Merchant/surface-specific QR campaigns and a simple merchant placard/staff guide are included.

The sprint is **technically launchable but operationally still open** because the planned Auto and Home pilot merchants have not yet been selected/recruited. The build intentionally refuses to invent or publicly activate those businesses.

## Public merchant activated
- Stevie's Bar & Grill — Eat & Drink
- Canonical route: `/local/stevies-bar-grill/`
- Perk: 20% off food and non-alcoholic drinks
- Redemption: show screen
- Insurance gate: none

## Physical campaign assets
Three independently attributable QR surfaces are included for Stevie's:
- merchant table;
- merchant counter;
- merchant placard.

A print-friendly SVG placard and staff redemption guide are included.

## Explicitly not claimed
- Auto pilot merchant recruitment is not complete.
- Home pilot merchant recruitment is not complete.
- No unnamed or synthetic merchant is published in either category.
- This archive does not claim in-person staff training or physical-device scan verification occurred; it provides the assets and runbook for those external launch steps.

## Continuation
Operationally close 408-LOCAL-1.9 only after the Auto and Home businesses are named, validated, and their exact merchant-owned terms are supplied. Only then advance to 408-LOCAL-1.10 certification.
